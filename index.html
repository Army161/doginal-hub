The Story of Doginal Dogs

What started as a simple tribute to one man's dog became a cultural movement on the Dogecoin blockchain.

"In a world where everything feels disposable, Doginal Dogs proves that some things are meant to last."

10,000 pixel-perfect dogs. Permanently inscribed. One pack. Infinite identity.

November 2023 — The Spark

Barkmeta simply wanted a pixel art version of his real dog, Atlas. Not about NFTs yet — just a creative tribute to his best friend. After many failed artist commissions, the idea evolved into something much bigger.

The Struggle

Finding the right artist was a journey. Commission after commission fell short. But persistence paid off — the vision was too powerful to abandon.

December 2023 — The Team Forms

Barkmeta teamed up with Willustrator (pixel artist) and Shibo (technical/community lead). Dogecoin inscriptions had started trending following the Bitcoin Ordinals wave.

Launch — 10,000 Dogs Live Forever

10,000 pixel-perfect dogs inscribed permanently on the Dogecoin blockchain. The result: a living brand, a global community, and a collection that stands the test of time.
